# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-29

First stable release.

- Switch between HDMI 1 and DisplayPort on a supported external main display.
- Open Helios from the menu bar or with Control–Option–H.
- Navigate inputs with arrow or number keys and confirm with Return.
- Choose immediate switching or a 5- or 10-second countdown, saved between launches.
- Show countdown progress with a green fill and contrasting text.
- Dismiss the switcher after five seconds of inactivity.
- Display the app version in the menu bar menu.
- Launch automatically at login by default, with a menu toggle and macOS approval handling.
- Build and install a standalone app into the user's Applications folder.
